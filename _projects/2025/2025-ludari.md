abstract: >-
  Ludari is a collection of fun, accessible minigames designed to support young learners while keeping play at the center. We believe that games can do more than entertain: they can build confidence, spark curiosity, and make learning feel exciting. Ludari bridges the gap between play and growth, offering experiences that feel rewarding for kids and reassuring for parents. Unlike one-size-fits-all apps, Ludari treats each game as a unique adventure. Some focus on focus, others on speed, and some are just about having a good laugh. Together, they create a library where every player can find something they enjoy. The app is designed for iOS devices, and while it has yet to be published on the App Store, a fully functional version has been developed and tested on XCode.
